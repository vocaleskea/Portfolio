LECTURE TASK 1

LECTURE TASK 1#

Instruction: Create a reaction paper (1 page) about the YouTube video titled “Machine Learning: Living in the Age of AI | A WIRED Film”, submit this in pdf format.

Use these as guide question for your paper.

-What are some of the most significant advancements in AI that the video highlights as having a positive impact on society?

-How does the video envision the future of AI, and what challenges and opportunities lie ahead?

-How can we strike a balance between harnessing the benefits of AI and addressing its potential drawbacks?

“Machine Learning: Living in the Age of AI | A WIRED Film” shows how artificial intelligence has already become part of everyday life. What stood out most were the positive applications of AI, such as its use in healthcare, where algorithms can help doctors diagnose diseases faster and more accurately, and in agriculture, where farmers use AI to monitor crops and improve harvests. The film also gives a glimpse of the future, where AI will be even more integrated into different aspects of society. It presents both opportunities and challenges. AI can support education, business, and even aging populations, but it also raises concerns about fairness, accountability, and how much we should rely on machines to make important decisions. This made me reflect on how AI is not just about technical progress but also about how we, as a society, choose to shape and control its impact.

The main takeaway for me is that there needs to be a balance. We should embrace AI for the good it can bring, but at the same time, we must ensure it is used responsibly. That means creating safeguards, being transparent about how it works, and making sure its benefits are shared widely. It is important to remember that technology should complement human values and decision-making, not replace them entirely.

Overall, the film left me feeling both inspired and cautious. AI has the potential to make life better in so many ways, but it will only fulfill that promise if we combine innovation with responsibility. It reminded me that the future of AI depends not only on what machines can do, but also on the choices humans make in guiding its development and use.
